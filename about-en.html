About RESOUL — Pet End-of-Life Care in Hong Kong

About RESOUL

Where every goodbye is filled
with love, dignity, and meaning.

Celebrate the bond. Remember, always.

Our founder — a passionate young lady — faced heartbreak after losing three beloved cats within a year. Each farewell exposed the same reality: many pet funeral experiences felt cold, rushed and impersonal, leaving families with only regret instead of memories.

Driven by this, she created RESOUL — a sanctuary where farewells become moments of comfort. Here, every ceremony is thoughtfully crafted: personalized, gentle and respectful, so each pet's unique story is honoured, not forgotten. Our modern memorials and caring service keep love and warmth at the heart of every goodbye.

Cherishing each story. Comforting every heart.

With us, partings aren't just the end — they are everlasting tributes, reminders of the joy and love you shared. When you reflect on your pet, may you feel a gentle smile rather than sorrow. Together, let's create beautiful endings to every story of love.

"The bond you share stays with you, always."

Service overview

One point of contact,
connecting support across every stage.

Each service is handled independently by the relevant professionals, while RESOUL helps you organise the timing, process and next step in advance — reducing repeated calls and last-minute decisions. Each service can connect together, or be chosen on its own.

🩺

Before the decision · Independent vet referral

We help organise your pet's information, check availability and coordinate; medical assessment and procedures are handled by an independent registered vet.

🕊️

The farewell · RESOUL farewell journey

Private pick-up, arrival notice, farewell ceremony, individual cremation, ashes and memorial arrangements.

🌱

After goodbye · Grief care

24–48 hour check-in, practical guides, a space to share, peer support and referral to professional counselling.

Our promise

Every step made clear,
so you never have to guess what's next.

🕯️

Private Journey

One stove, one pet, every time. We close the door and light a flame that belongs only to your darling. With no other souls sharing the space, their final journey stays hushed, honoured and utterly theirs.

🎥

Zero Mistake

We trace, photograph and film every gentle step, and give you a private portal to watch their whole journey — from arrival to ashes — in crystal clarity. This isn't a procedure; it's lasting peace of mind. Even one tear from a mix-up is one too many.

🐾

Every Farewell Helps a Soul

We stand with those who care for animals in need. With each service we support our pet-charity partners and offer a dignified cremation to a stray — so your darling's final journey helps transform another little life, turning parting into a lasting embrace.

Founder Vivian Chong and the RESOUL team, together with trained drivers and partner professionals, safeguard every journey. Each vet and support partner uses only verified names, qualifications and registration; medical and psychological services are provided independently by the relevant professionals.
