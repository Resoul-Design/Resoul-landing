Professional Support & Referral for Pet Loss | RESOUL

Professional referral

When you need more support,
we'll help you find the right person.

If grief keeps affecting your sleep, work, daily life or relationships, RESOUL can — with your consent — help refer you to a qualified and experienced counsellor, counselling psychologist, clinical psychologist or suitable organisation. Professionals assess, provide services and charge fees independently; we do not share your pet or health information without consent.

🤝

Counselling & psychology

Counselling psychologists, clinical psychologists or registered social workers, to support you through grief, sleeplessness and distress.

🕊️

Pet-loss support

Pet-loss grief support groups or peer communities, a space where you're understood as you slowly find your footing.

🏥

Medical & mental health

If needed, a family doctor or psychiatrist can provide further assessment and follow-up.

Partnership referral

Service referral with HKU psychological support

RESOUL is establishing a service-referral arrangement with a psychological support unit at the University of Hong Kong (HKU), so families who need it can connect to professional support more smoothly. The signed referral document will be shown here.

Crisis note

If you are having thoughts of harming yourself or others, cannot keep yourself safe, or are in immediate danger, please call 999, go to the nearest A&E, or contact a Hong Kong crisis service (The Samaritans 24h 2389 2222). This page and RESOUL's general care do not replace emergency mental-health or medical services.
