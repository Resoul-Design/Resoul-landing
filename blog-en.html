Privacy Policy · Terms of Use · 中文

Disclaimer　RESOUL provides pet end-of-life arrangements, cremation, farewell ceremonies and memorial services. Veterinary assessment and euthanasia are carried out by an independent registered veterinarian who issues the relevant receipts; RESOUL does not provide veterinary medical services. Prices and information on this site are for reference only; actual arrangements, fees and timelines may vary by individual case and are confirmed in writing before proceeding. The AI chat and letter-writing features offer emotional companionship and help organising words only — they are not psychotherapy or professional counselling; if grief continues to affect daily life, please seek help from a registered psychologist, social worker or doctor. In an emergency, call your local emergency services.
