Farewell journey

Finish the words
left unsaid, slowly.

A farewell shaped by your love and your story.

When every farewell deserves to be cherished, this is more than a cremation — it is a final tribute to your companion's life. From private pick-up, gentle grooming, a farewell ceremony and individual cremation, to ashes and memorial arrangements, every step is followed by a dedicated person, so you can say goodbye clearly, quietly and without being rushed.

Losing your beloved pet is one of the deepest heartbreaks we can face. They were never just a companion — they were family. Allow yourself to grieve slowly, and lean on those who understand when you need to.

At RESOUL, your companion is cared for by a team with years of experience across veterinary support and pet cremation. We keep the process calm, transparent and personal, so each family can make decisions with enough space and support.

Good to know

A few things to note,
before and after cremation.

This helps you feel prepared. If anything is unclear, WhatsApp us anytime and we'll arrange each step with you.

📝

Tell us before pick-up

Your pet's weight, location and time of passing, so we can arrange a suitable pick-up and time slot.

🩺

Medical implants

Please let us know of any microchip, metal implant or pacemaker in advance; some must be removed before cremation for safety.

🧊

Keeping them for now

If immediate pick-up isn't possible, wrap them in a towel and refrigerate or keep them in a cool, shaded place, away from direct sun.

💐

Farewell items

You may prepare a few small tokens (flowers, a letter, a treat) to send with them; metal, glass, electronics and non-flammable items cannot go in.

⏳

Timing

Individual cremation usually takes a few hours; we'll confirm the ceremony time and the return of ashes with you.

🏺

Ashes & keepsakes

Ashes are roughly 3–5% of body weight (depending on bone density); choose collection, delivery, temporary keeping or a return-to-nature arrangement.

What you may bring on the day

  • Fresh flowers
  • Their favourite treats or snacks
  • Their everyday leash or collar
  • A beloved toy or small blanket
  • A family photo or a meaningful keepsake
  • Music, photos or a short video to play
  • A letter you'd like to read to them

None of these are required — what matters most is the presence of you and your family. If you'd like any items cremated together, or wish to keep fur, the collar or other small tokens, please confirm with the team before the ceremony.

When your pet has just passed

In a quiet spot at home, arrange a comfortable box for your pet with a towel they loved. After about two hours the body stiffens; you can gently position them and cover them. Control the room temperature (air-con in summer), place ice packs near the head and belly, and add toys, food and photos. Then call the Resoul 24-hour line 6476 2951 as soon as possible; staff arrive within two hours to bring your pet in for grooming. You may groom or wipe them, or offer blessings.

Resoul offers 24-hour pickup, from home or any vet clinic in Hong Kong. The fee covers all-district pickup (Discovery Bay +HK$300; walk-up building stairs incur a surcharge). Share your pet's condition in advance to speed things up.

Cats, dogs, rabbits, hamsters, birds and other animals — Resoul welcomes them all; they are all treasured.
