Home farewell · Independent vet referral

A gentle goodbye in the
place they know best.

For some elderly, seriously ill or travel-anxious pets, being assessed by a vet at home can ease the strain of travelling and the unease of unfamiliar surroundings. At the owner's request, RESOUL can help contact an independent registered veterinarian offering home visits, and coordinate the pick-up and farewell arrangements that follow.

When to reach out

When the good days
grow fewer than the hard ones.

If your pet is in persistent pain, has difficulty breathing or eating, shows a clear decline in mobility, or the good days are growing fewer than the hard ones, you can first contact your own primary vet or an independent registered vet for a professional assessment. Observing quality of life is only to help organise the situation — it does not mean euthanasia is necessarily appropriate.

Important notice

RESOUL is not a veterinary clinic and does not provide veterinary diagnosis, treatment, prescription, medication supply or euthanasia procedures. All medical assessment, advice, medication and procedures are the sole responsibility of, and performed by, an independent registered veterinarian holding a valid practising certificate. The vet retains full professional discretion to advise against or not proceed after assessment. Vet service hours and final fees are confirmed by the vet.

If your pet shows extreme breathing difficulty, seizures, severe bleeding, loss of consciousness or any other emergency, please contact the nearest 24-hour animal hospital immediately — do not wait for a regular appointment.
